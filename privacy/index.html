Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 29, 2026

Clean Wash ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Clean Wash mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

When you use Clean Wash, we may collect the following information:

  • Account Information — Your name, email address, and phone number when you create an account.
  • Booking Information — Details about the car wash services you book, including dates, times, and service selections.
  • Device Information — Device type, operating system version, and a unique device identifier for push notifications.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and manage your car wash bookings
  • Send booking confirmations and appointment reminders
  • Improve and maintain the app
  • Respond to your questions or requests

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • With the car wash facility to fulfill your booking
  • When required by law or to protect our legal rights

Data Storage and Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data. Your information is stored securely and we retain it only as long as necessary to provide our services.

Push Notifications

With your permission, we send push notifications for booking reminders and updates. You can disable notifications at any time through your device settings.

Your Rights

You may request to access, update, or delete your personal information at any time by contacting us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes through the app or by email.
