Support

How do I book a car wash?

Open the Clean Wash app, choose your preferred service, pick a date and time, and confirm your booking. You'll receive a confirmation right away.

Can I cancel or reschedule my booking?

Yes. Open the app, go to your upcoming bookings, and tap on the appointment you'd like to change. You can cancel or reschedule from there.

How do I turn off notifications?

Go to your iPhone's Settings, scroll down to Clean Wash, tap Notifications, and toggle them off.
